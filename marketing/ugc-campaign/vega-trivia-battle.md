# VEGA — Warlord of Trivia Battle (Team vs Team)

## Character bible

- **Look:** mid-20s woman, New York. Hair bleached yellow and sculpted into
  literal upward flames/spikes. Blue/purple checkered chunky knit, black
  mini skirt, boots, iced americano. Resting expression: about to draft you.
- **Voice:** intense sports-coach energy applied to trivia with zero irony.
  Calls teammates "roster." Calls losing "film to study." Insists on being
  called **King**, not Queen — "Queen is a chess piece. King is the win
  condition." (Comment-section catnip.)
- **Domain:** **Trivia Battle** — two teams face off over a board of priced
  categories. Vega treats category selection like an NFL draft board.
- **Camera grammar:** 0.5× selfie strides through brick-and-glass NYC
  streets, locker-room-speech framing, whip pans, harder cuts than the
  other three. Golden hour but sharper shadows.
- **Recurring bits:** recruiting strangers mid-video; a tiny notebook
  labeled **ROSTER**; pointing at the camera on "you."
- **Catchphrase:** *"I don't play trivia. I run a franchise."*

## PHASE 1 — CORONATION

### V1 — "I scouted my barista"
**Format:** recruitment comedy, ~22s · **Sound:** sports anthem
**Hook (0–2s):** "This man just named three capitals while steaming milk. He doesn't know it yet, but he's mine."
**Beats:**
1. Coffee shop. Barista casually corrects a customer about a country fact. Vega slowly lowers her cup. Whip pan to her face: draft mode.
2. "Sir. Do you have plans tonight at 9?" Barista: "…making coffee?" — "Wrong. You're my Geography seat."
3. Cut: team lobby screen, five players locked. Text: *"roster complete"*
4. To cam: "Some people collect sneakers. I collect specialists."
**Caption:** always be scouting. #FourCrowns
**Why it works:** recruit-a-stranger is an endlessly rerunnable street format and establishes her entire personality in one video.

### V2 — "The category board is a war map"
**Format:** whiteboard tactics parody, ~24s · **Sound:** war-room drums
**Beats:**
1. Hook: "You pick trivia categories with your heart. That's why you lose."
2. Vega at an actual whiteboard, drawing arrows over category tiles: "High-price History? That's not a question, that's a trap. They WANT you greedy."
3. "You take the cheap Sports tile first. Why? Momentum. Confidence is a stat."
4. "Then, when their morale dips—" slams marker "—you take the expensive tile they were saving. That's not trivia. That's psychological warfare with a scoreboard."
**Caption:** priced categories = priced mistakes 📋
**Why it works:** actually teaches the mode's mechanic (priced category board) while being completely unhinged.

### V3 — "Cutting my best friend from the roster"
**Format:** mock-serious sit-down, ~20s · **Sound:** sad piano → team chant
**Beats:**
1. Hook: "This is the hardest conversation a King can have."
2. Across the table, her best friend. Vega: "Dana. Your movie knowledge? Elite. Your buzzer discipline?" Slides phone over: stats. "You answered 'DiCaprio' to a science question."
3. Dana: "It was about ice." Vega, closing her eyes: "…reinstated."
4. Text: *"management is lonely"*
**Caption:** the roster is a living document
**Why it works:** friendship-as-sports-franchise is the bit fans will quote; "it was about ice" is engineered to be the pinned comment.

## PHASE 4 — THE CROWN MATCH

### V10 — "I got the letter."
**Format:** synchronized lore event, ~16s · **Sound:** epic trailer (same as all accounts)
**Beats:**
1. Hook: gold envelope taped to her gym locker. She opens it with one rip.
2. Card: crown icon. *"One crown. Four pretenders. First to 6 wins."* Her face: this is Christmas.
3. "1v1?" Laughs once. Stops. "…1v1." The smile dies. Text: *"no roster. no film. no franchise."*

**Caption:** Friday. no subs. @mytrivia
**Why it works:** the format strips each character of their superpower — hers most of all, and her audience knows it.

### V11 — "Training alone (this is miserable)"

**Beats:**

**Caption:** the franchise is one woman this week. #FourCrowns
**Why it works:** the comedy-to-sincerity flip is the highest-completion-rate structure on TikTok; sets her finale stakes.

### V12 — "THE CROWN MATCH — Vega's cut"
**Format:** finale, ~35s · **Sound:** stadium anthem → heartbeat
**Beats:**
1. Hook: four crowns, one frame. Text: *"this actually happened"*
2. Vega's cut is shot like a title fight: walkout energy, cracking knuckles, trash talk at three people who are just… sitting there.
3. She starts 3–1 up — instinct is real. Then the hard logic questions bite: the one-minute think timer, no roster to lean on. 5–5.
4. Match point: Juno takes it. Vega stands, crosses the room… and shakes Juno's hand. To cam: "I found my superstar. Recruitment continues."
5. Final card: *"There was never one king. There's a crown in the app — and anyone can take it."* @mytrivia.
**Caption:** Season 2: the DYNASTY begins. she WILL say yes. #FourCrowns
**Why it works:** her loss converts into the Season 2 engine (recruiting Juno) and models the app's real loop — lose, requeue, run it back.
