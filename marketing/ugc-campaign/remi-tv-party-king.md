# REMI — King of the Living Room (TV Party)

## Character bible

- **Look:** early-20s guy, LA. Hair sculpted and dyed into a solid yellow
  crown shape. Blue/purple checkered knit sweater, light-wash jeans, iced
  latte permanently in hand. Glowing golden-retriever grin.
- **Voice:** relentlessly positive host energy. Talks to the camera like it's
  his co-host. Physically incapable of letting a room be bored. His villain
  origin story: a silent house party.
- **Domain:** **TV Party** — one big screen, everyone's phone is a
  controller. Remi believes hosting is a sacred calling and he is its king.
- **Camera grammar:** 0.5× ultra-wide selfie, golden hour, walking shots on
  Rodeo-style streets or living rooms mid-party. Warm, bright, saturated.
- **Recurring props:** the TV lobby QR/join code on a big screen, his latte,
  a remote he never needs ("phones ARE the remote").
- **Catchphrase:** *"A living room is just a stadium that hasn't met me yet."*

## PHASE 1 — CORONATION

### V1 — "POV: you invited Remi to a 'chill' hangout"
**Format:** POV comedy, ~22s · **Sound:** trending suspense → party drop
**Hook (0–2s):** Remi, deadly serious into 0.5× cam: "You said *chill*. I heard *challenge*."
**Beats:**
1. Friend (off-cam): "We're just gonna watch a movie." Remi nods slowly, sips latte.
2. Smash cut: the TV shows a MyTrivia TV Party lobby, six phones lighting up on the couch. On-screen text: *"movie night: cancelled"*
3. Rapid cuts of friends screaming answers, one guy standing on the couch, someone's mom locked in.
4. End on Remi whispering to camera while chaos rages behind: "They always say chill. They never mean it."
**Caption:** every party has a king. some parties have ME. #FourCrowns
**Why it works:** POV format + relatable "hijacked hangout" premise; the app is set dressing, not subject.
