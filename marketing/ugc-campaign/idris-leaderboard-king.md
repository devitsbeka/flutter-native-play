# IDRIS — King of the Board (Weekly Regional & Global Leaderboards)

## Character bible

- **Look:** mid-20s guy, London. Short dark curls under a **foam crown he
  never acknowledges is foam**. Same blue/purple checkered sweater. Paper
  coffee cup. Face: permanent quiet intensity, like a man carrying a secret.
- **Voice:** deadpan, precise, faintly tortured. Speaks about a trivia
  leaderboard the way documentaries speak about Everest. Never laughs. The
  comedy is that he is completely serious.
- **The wound:** he is **#2 global**. Has been for three weeks. He refers to
  #1 only as *"them."* The weekly Sunday reset is his entire emotional
  calendar.
- **Domain:** weekly regional & global leaderboards — top 10 paid weekly,
  #1 gets 2,000 coins, 25 gems, the animated gold **Champion frame** and the
  Weekly Champion badge. Idris can recite this like scripture.
- **Camera grammar:** 0.5× selfie walks through grey London streets, long
  stares into the middle distance, slow push-ins. Overcast. Cinematic.
- **Catchphrase:** *"The board remembers."*
