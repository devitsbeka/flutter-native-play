# JUNO — The Oracle (Guess the Logo · Celebrities · Guess the City)

## Character bible

- **Look:** early-20s woman, Seoul. Green-yellow curls twisted into little
  star/crown points. Blue/purple checkered sweater, black skirt, small gold
  hoops, iced americano. Expression: serene, warm — and then the eyes lock on.
- **Voice:** soft, sweet, a little sing-song… delivering answers with the
  finality of a court verdict. Never brags. Never rushes. The comedy is the
  gap between the gentle delivery and the terrifying accuracy.
- **Superpower:** recognition. Logos from a 2-pixel corner. Celebrities from
  an elbow. Cities from the shape of a shadow. She doesn't "know" answers —
  she *remembers* them, like they already happened.
- **Domain:** the guess categories — **Guess the Logo, Celebrities, Guess
  the City** (plus flags when she's showing off).
- **Camera grammar:** 0.5× selfie on busy Seoul shopping streets, clean
  light, softer cuts than the others. Answers are always one take, no jump
  cut on the reveal (the no-cut proof IS the format).
- **Signature move:** answer → tiny smile → sip. In that order. Always.
- **Catchphrase:** *"I don't guess."*

## PHASE 1 — CORONATION

### V1 — "Don't show me the logo. Show me a corner."
**Format:** challenge demo, ~20s · **Sound:** minimal click track, sounds stop on each answer
**Hook (0–2s):** "People say the logo round is easy. Okay. Cover 95% of it."
**Beats:**
1. Friend holds phone with a logo question, papered over except one corner. Juno, instantly: correct. Smile. Sip.
2. Three more, faster, less visible each time. Last one is literally a sliver of a curve. Correct. The friend checks the phone, looks at the camera like it's The Office.
3. Friend: "How." Juno, gently: "I don't guess."
4. Text: *"attempt #1 of proving she's human: failed"*
**Caption:** send harder ones. I'll wait. #FourCrowns
**Why it works:** one-take proof format = "is this real?" comments = engagement; the friend's disbelief gives viewers their surrogate.

### V2 — "Guessing cities from strangers' vacation photos"
**Format:** interactive street format, ~24s · **Sound:** soft lo-fi
**Beats:**
1. Hook: "Strangers show me one vacation photo. I tell them where they were. No landmarks allowed."
2. Stranger 1 shows a photo of a random alley. Juno: "Lisbon. The pavement gives it away." Stranger's jaw drops.
3. Stranger 2: a plate of food. "…Osaka?" Stranger: "HOW." Juno: "The plate."
4. Stranger 3 tries to trick her with a photo of the street they're standing on. Juno, without looking up: "Nice try. And you took it this morning."
**Caption:** the pavement always talks 🗺️
**Why it works:** street-interview format is a proven engine, and "no landmarks" makes her power feel supernatural rather than nerdy.

### V3 — "My grandmother made me like this"
**Format:** origin-story storytime, ~22s · **Sound:** nostalgic piano
**Beats:**
1. Hook: "Everyone asks how I do it. It's my grandmother's fault."
2. "Every night, TV quiz shows. She never let me see the screen. I sat behind the couch and had to answer from the *audio*. She said watching was cheating."
3. "By nine I could name a celebrity from their laugh. By eleven, a city from its traffic sound." Beat. "By twelve she was betting on me against the neighbors."
4. Warm smile: "She's still undefeated as a manager." Text: *"the first Oracle was her"*
**Caption:** all crowns are inherited somehow 👑
**Why it works:** origin lore humanizes the superpower and gives grandma a recurring cameo slot (fans WILL demand grandma content).
